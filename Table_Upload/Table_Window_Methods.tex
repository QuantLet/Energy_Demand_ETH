Analysis & Quantity & Question addressed & Method \\
\midrule

Isolated
& ETC increment (TH/s)
& How sensitive is the estimated migration increment
to the observation windows and hash-rate statistic?
& Calculate post-minus-pre ETC hash rate under each
window combination using the mean, median, or
10\% trimmed mean. \\

Isolated
& Pre-Merge power (GW)
& How does variation in the migration increment
affect estimated pre-Merge power?
& Reallocate cohort hash rates using each increment
and recompute power, holding profitability thresholds,
hardware weights, tariff, and overhead fixed. \\

Isolated
& Fixed-budget saving (\%)
& Does the accounting reduction depend on the
window used to measure migration?
& Calculate $100(1-\mathcal B_{\mathrm{ref}}/P_{0,k})$,
using window-specific power and the fixed
reference four-chain budget. \\

Synchronized
& Synchronized budget (GW)
& How sensitive is the destination electricity budget
to the post-Merge revenue window?
& Recalculate mean daily revenues for all four chains
over each selected post-window and convert them
to electricity budgets at the reference tariff. \\

Synchronized
& Synchronized saving (\%)
& How does jointly varying migration and revenues
affect the accounting reduction?
& Calculate $100(1-\mathcal B_k/P_{0,k})$,
using window-specific migration and revenues
while retaining reference hardware classification. \\

Reclassified
& Reclassified saving (\%)
& How does additionally updating hardware
classification affect the accounting reduction?
& Update the ETC profitability threshold over
the selected post-window, reclassify hardware,
recompute power, and calculate
$100(1-\mathcal B_k/P^{*}_{0,k})$. \\
